The poles of X[z] are 1, 3 + j4, and 3 − j4. Whenever there are complex-conjugate poles, the
problem can be worked out in two ways. In the first method we expand X[z] into (modified)
first-order partial fractions. In the second method, rather than obtain one factor corresponding
to each complex-conjugate pole, we obtain quadratic factors corresponding to each pair of
complex-conjugate poles. This procedure is explained next.
