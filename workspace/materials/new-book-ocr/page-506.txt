      5.2-3 z-Domain Differentiation Property (Multiplication by n)
      Multiplying a signal by n in the time domain produces differentiation in the z-domain. That is, if

                                                  x[n]u[n] ⇐⇒ X[z]

      then
                                                                         d
                                               nx[n]u[n] ⇐⇒ −z              X[z]                   (5.18)
                                                                         dz
      Proof.


                                                           ∞
                                          d            d "
                                     −z      X[z] = −z        x[n]z−n
                                          dz           dz n=0
                                                        ∞
                                                        "
                                                 = −z            −nx[n]z−n−1
                                                           n=0
                                                     ∞
                                                     "
                                                 =         nx[n]z−n = Z{nx[n]u[n]}
                                                     n=0

      5.2-4 Time-Reversal Property

      then†
                                                  x[−n] ⇐⇒ X[1/z]
