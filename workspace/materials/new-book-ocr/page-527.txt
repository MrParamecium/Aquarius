system to a discrete-time sinusoidal input of frequency  is also a discrete-time sinusoid of the
same frequency. The amplitude of the output sinusoid is |H[ej ]| times the input amplitude, and
the phase of the output sinusoid is shifted by  H[ej ] with respect to the input phase. Clearly,

versus  shows how the system modifies or shifts the phase of the input sinusoid. Note that
H[ej ] incorporates the information of both amplitude and phase responses and therefore is called
the frequency responses of the system.
