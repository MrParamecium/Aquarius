In this manner, we can compute the frequency response H[ej ] for any value of  by selecting the
point on the unit circle at an angle . This point is ej . To compute the frequency response H[ej ],
we connect all poles and zeros to this point and use the foregoing equations to determine |H[ej ]|
and  H[ej ]. We repeat this procedure for all values of  from 0 to π to obtain the frequency
response.
