      5.8-2 Using the Bilateral z-Transform for Analysis of LTID Systems
      Because the bilateral z-transform can handle noncausal signals, we can use this transform to
      analyze noncausal linear systems. The zero-state response y[n] is given by


                                               y[n] = Z −1 {X[z]H[z]}


      provided X[z]H[z] exists. The ROC of X[z]H[z] is the region in which both X[z] and H[z] exist,
      which means that the region is the common part of the ROC of both X[z] and H[z].
