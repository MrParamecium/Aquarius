      R ELATIONSHIP B ETWEEN h[n] AND H[z]
      For an LTID system, if h[n] is its unit impulse response, then from Eq. (3.39), where we defined
      H[z], the system transfer function, we write

                                                        ∞
                                                        "
                                               H[z] =          h[n]z−n                             (5.11)
                                                        n=−∞


      For causal systems, the limits on the sum are from n = 0 to ∞. This equation shows that the
      transfer function H[z] is the z-transform of the impulse response h[n] of an LTID system; that is,

                                                  h[n] ⇐⇒ H[z]

      This important result relates the time-domain specification h[n] of a system to H[z], the
      frequency-domain specification of a system. The result is parallel to that for LTIC systems.
