5.10-5 Bilinear Transformation with Prewarping
      The bilinear transformation maps the entire infinite-length ω axis onto the finite-length unit circle
      (z = ej ) according to ω = (2/T) tan (/2) (see Prob. 5.6-18b). Equivalently,  = 2 arctan(ωT/2).
      The nonlinearity of the tangent function causes a frequency compression, commonly called
      frequency warping, that distorts the transformation.

      lowpass filter with cutoff frequency ωc = 2π 3000 rad/s. If the target digital system uses a sampling
