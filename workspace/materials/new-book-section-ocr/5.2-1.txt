understanding of the shift property remains mechanical rather than intuitive or heuristic. For this
reason, using a hypothetical signal x[n], we have illustrated various shifted signals for k = 1 in

              The signal x[n] can be expressed as a product of n and a gate pulse u[n] − u[n − 6].
          Therefore,

                                     x[n] = n{u[n] − u[n − 6]} = nu[n] − nu[n − 6]

          We cannot find the z-transform of nu[n − 6] directly by using the right-shift property
          [Eq. (5.13)]. So we rearrange it in terms of (n − 6)u[n − 6] as follows:

                                      x[n] = nu[n] − (n − 6 + 6)u[n − 6]
                                           = nu[n] − (n − 6)u[n − 6] − 6u[n − 6]
