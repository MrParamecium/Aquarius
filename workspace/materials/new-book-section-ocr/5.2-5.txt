5.2-5 Convolution Property
The time-convolution property states that if‡

                            x1 [n] ⇐⇒ X1 [z]           and            x2 [n] ⇐⇒ X2 [z],

then (time convolution)
                                      x1 [n] ∗ x2 [n] ⇐⇒ X1 [z]X2 [z]                           (5.19)

Proof. This property applies to causal as well as noncausal sequences. We shall prove it for the
more general case of noncausal sequences, where the convolution sum ranges from −∞ to ∞.
